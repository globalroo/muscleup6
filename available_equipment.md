Below is a **7-day table** you can incorporate into your booklet or printouts. Each **day** has its own section. For **resistance days**, the table lists recommended exercises, suggested sets/reps, and a **“Recommended Weekly Increment”** so you know roughly how much to increase the load each week—assuming good form and recovery. For **cardio days**, the table outlines either **intervals** (HIIT) or **steady-state** targets, plus ideas for progression.

> **How to Use**:  
> - Print or copy each table for the **4-week phase** you’re in (Phase 1, 2, or 3), or expand columns if you’d like to track all 12 weeks on a single sheet.  
> - In the **“Week # Weight/Performance”** columns, record exactly what you did (weight used, total intervals, time, etc.).  
> - Follow the **Recommended Weekly Increment** guidelines as a rough progression plan. If you stall or feel overly fatigued, maintain the same weight/performance until you’re ready to increase.

# General Increments & Progression Tips

1. **Upper Body Compound** (Bench Press, Overhead Press, Rows): **+2.5–5 lb** per week is typical. If your weights are in kg, aim for **+1–2 kg**.  
2. **Lower Body Compound** (Squat, Deadlift, RDL): **+5–10 lb** (2–5 kg) per week is typical, or slower if you’re near your strength limit.  
3. **Accessory Exercises** (biceps/triceps, calf raises, lunges): smaller jumps **(+2–5 lb)** or add 1–2 reps if you can’t easily go up in weight.  
4. **Cardio**: Increase total intervals (HIIT) or add 2–5 minutes to steady-state each week; or increase speed if you’re time-limited.
